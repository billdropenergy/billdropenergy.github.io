How It Works
How do we break out HVAC from a single utility bill?

Every owner asks this — and it's the right question. Your utility bill shows one number: total kWh consumed. It tells you nothing about which system used what.

BillDrop uses the U.S. Department of Energy Commercial Building Energy Consumption Survey (CBECS) — the same allocation model the EPA uses for its ENERGY STAR program — to estimate what percentage of your bill each major system accounts for, calibrated to your specific building type and climate zone.

We then cross-reference your actual kWh against the regional benchmark (38.4 kWh/sqft/yr for Denver's Mountain zone) to identify which system is causing the overage and by exactly how much. Every dollar figure in your report is traceable back to your bills and to a publicly available federal data source.
